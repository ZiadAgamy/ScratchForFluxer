Add a bot

Bring a bot you made in Fluxer. Paste its token and you can start building with blocks. New to this? Follow the step-by-step guide.

  1. 1

    Create a bot in Fluxer

    Open Fluxer, go to your User Settings and find Applications. Create an application, then add a bot to it.

    {{ icon("arrow-square-out") }}Open Fluxer
  2. 2

    Copy its token

    In your application's settings, press Regenerate next to the bot token and copy the new token. Treat it like a password: whoever has it can control your bot.

  3. 3

    Paste it here

    We check it with Fluxer first. After that it is kept encrypted, and it is never shown again.
